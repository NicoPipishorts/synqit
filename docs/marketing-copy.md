## How it works

**Section pill:** Mobile-first flow

### Step 1 — Host sets up the playlist

Connect your Spotify or Apple Music account, name the playlist, add an optional description, and Synqit creates it directly in your provider library. A magic link is generated instantly.

### Step 2 — Guests add tracks

Guests open the link on any device — no download, no account, no friction. They search the host provider's full catalog and add songs with a single tap.

### Step 3 — Host curates live

Watch contributions arrive in real time. Remove anything that doesn't fit. Close the playlist when it's ready. Full control, start to finish.

## Why hosts choose Synqit

**Section description:** Structured for clarity and speed — create, share, collect tracks, moderate.

### Host-first setup

Connect your streaming provider, create the playlist, and copy the magic link in under a minute. No configuration, no complexity.

### Guest-friendly by design

Guests open the link, search tracks, and contribute without creating an account. The lowest-friction contribution flow that exists.

### Provider-aligned

Tracks are searched against and added directly to the host's provider library. No separate storage, no duplicate catalogs — everything ends up exactly where it belongs.

### Live moderation

See who added what, when, and from which source. Remove tracks mid-event. Revoke the link at any time and regenerate a new one. You stay in control.

## Feature highlights

### Magic link

One URL is all guests need. No app. No signup. No instructions. Share it over SMS, email, Instagram, or a QR code at the door. Works on every device.

### Spotify + Apple Music

Synqit connects to both major streaming providers. Hosts pick the one they use — guests search and contribute from that exact catalog. No cross-platform compromise.

### Real-time activity feed

Your dashboard shows every track added, the time it was added, and who added it (guest or provider sync). Track the energy of your event as it builds.

### Revocable links

Need to stop accepting contributions? Revoke the magic link instantly. Generate a fresh one to share with a new group. The playlist stays — only access changes.

### Open / Closed states

When the event wraps up, close the playlist. Guests can still browse the final track list, but the submission window is shut. Permanent. Intentional. Clean.

### Draft auto-save

Start creating a playlist and get interrupted? Synqit auto-saves your progress every few seconds. Pick up exactly where you left off.

### PWA — installable

Add Synqit to your home screen for instant access during events. No App Store required. One tap to your dashboard, playlists, and live activity.

## Tone of voice

- **Direct.** No padding, no buzzwords. Say exactly what the product does.
- **Confident but not arrogant.** The product solves a real problem cleanly — let that speak.
- **Guest-empathetic.** Always frame friction from the guest's perspective first.
- **Active verbs.** Create, share, collect, moderate, contribute, revoke. Not passive.
- **Short sentences.** Especially on mobile. One idea per line.

## What Synqit is NOT (avoid in copy)

- Not a music streaming service
- Not a DJ tool or full setlist manager
- Not a social network or music discovery app
- Not a replacement for Spotify/Apple Music — it works _with_ them
